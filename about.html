01ABOUTThe editor behind the frame

Editing is where the story finds its rhythm.

I’m Subas Ghimire, a Video Editor / Visual Editor at Kantipur Television.

I work across news and talk-show formats, bringing together footage, sound, graphics and timing to make each story clear and engaging. Most days begin with rushes and a deadline, and end with a cut that has to be accurate enough for broadcast.

That pace taught me the part of editing that never shows on screen: knowing which two seconds to keep, when a graphic helps the story and when it only decorates it, and how sound carries an audience from one shot to the next. I’m always exploring better workflows and new post-production techniques.
