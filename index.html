06FAQBefore you write
What kind of video work do you take on?

Broadcast news and talk-show editing, motion and broadcast graphics in After Effects, colour and audio finishing, and vertical short-form edits for social media.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I edit in-house at Kantipur Television in Kathmandu and take on remote projects — footage can arrive as a Drive, WeTransfer or FTP link.

Which software do you use?

Adobe Premiere Pro for editing, After Effects for motion graphics and titles, and Photoshop for thumbnails and image work.

How long does an edit take?

News segments can move the same day. Longer pieces depend on footage volume, graphics and revisions — send the deadline with the brief and I will tell you what is realistic.

How do I start a project?

Use the contact form below or email ghimiresubas347@gmail.com with the footage link, target length, deadline and any references.
