Broadcast editing

Daily news packages

Kantipur Television · Kathmandu — current role

SAME-DAY TURNAROUND

The brief

A newsroom runs on the clock. Rushes land, the script changes, and the package still has to be accurate, clear and ready for the bulletin.

What I do

  • Cut rushes to the script and to the pictures, so the story still reads with the sound off.
  • Add lower-thirds, name straps and the supporting graphics the story actually needs.
  • Level the audio, check the final file and hand it over ready to air.

Delivered

Broadcast-ready packages, inside the day's deadline.

02
Studio & programme

Talk shows & studio programmes

Kantipur Television · Global Television HD

EPISODE-LENGTH

The brief

A long conversation has to feel shorter than it is — without losing the argument, the pauses or the guest's voice.

What I do

  • Find the spine of the conversation, then cut around it.
  • Keep the programme's look consistent — titles, lower-thirds, transitions, timing.
  • Balance audio between speakers and clean up the joins.

Delivered

Episode-length programme files, ready for broadcast.

03
Post-production

Broadcast motion graphics

After Effects · news and programme output

MOTION & TYPE

The brief

A graphic earns its place by explaining something. If it only decorates the frame, it gets cut.

What I do

  • Build titles, lower-thirds, tickers and full-frame graphics.
  • Keep type legible at broadcast size and consistent with the channel's look.
  • Match the animation's timing to the cut, not to the clock.

Delivered

Rendered graphics and project files, ready to drop into the timeline.

Short-form

Vertical cut-downs

Shorts · Reels · TikTok

9:16

The brief

The same story, framed for a phone. The first second has to hold someone who never chose to watch.

What I do

  • Re-frame long-form pieces to 9:16 and keep the subject inside the safe area.
  • Tighten the pace, front-load the hook and caption where it helps.
  • Export platform-ready files at the right duration and bitrate.

Delivered

Vertical cuts sized for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.
